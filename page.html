Hub settings

How every rank, projection and tool on this hub is calculated.

Stats source

  • Player stats: Hashtagbasketball Fantasy Basketball Rankings, 2026-27 per-game averages, our 9 categories only (FG%, FT%, 3PTM, PTS, REB, AST, ST, BLK, TO).
  • Player value = Hashtagbasketball's 9-cat total.
  • Refreshed every Monday. Last refresh: —.
  • Players outside Hashtagbasketball's top 200: —. They keep their previous per-game estimate and rank below #200.
  • Rosters, standings and scores: Yahoo, pasted into the Google Sheet. NBA games: official NBA schedule. Injuries and news: daily.

Power rankings

  • Counts up to 13 players per team: the 10 starters and 3 bench spots.
  • Players in an IL slot are not counted. Same rule for every team.
  • Rank = categories won per week: each team's expected category wins against each of the other 9 teams, averaged.
  • Ties broken by playoff odds.
  • Playoff odds: the rest of the regular season (through Week 19) played 2,000 times from the current Yahoo standings. Odds = share of seasons finishing top 6. Projected record = average record.
  • Each simulated week allows for swings in form (weekly and season-long), so odds are not all-or-nothing.

Category ranks

  • Same counted players as power rankings (no IL players).
  • Counting stats: sum of per-game averages. FG% = total FGM ÷ total FGA; FT% = total FTM ÷ total FTA.
  • 1 = best. TO: fewest is best.
  • Strong = 1st–3rd · Even = 4th–7th · Weak = 8th–10th.
  • Win chance per category uses typical weekly variation: —.

Category profile and team cards

  • Radar (Rosters tab) shows the category ranks above. Outer edge = 1st, dashed ring = league middle.
  • Team card name = a 3-word nickname for the team's playstyle.
  • Green pill = 3–4 strongest categories by current rank. Red pill = 3–4 weakest by current rank (IL players not counted).
  • Description = playstyle with every player healthy: Core, Swing and Underdog categories.
  • Category ranks table: Cats/wk = projected categories won per week now (IL excluded). Healthy = the same with IL players back (best 13 of the full roster); bold when it beats Cats/wk by 0.15 or more.
  • Description = best and last categories only.

Weekly matchup prediction

  • Uses the real NBA schedule for the week.
  • Each game day, the 10 highest-value players with a game start.
  • Injured players sit until their expected return date. IL players count only from their return date.
  • Projected totals = sum of per-game stats across all starts.
  • If a week's schedule isn't loaded, it compares per-game averages instead.

Category edge

  • Compares the two projected weekly totals in each category.
  • Toss-up if counting stats are within 3%, FG% within .0036, FT% within .0075.
  • Otherwise the edge goes to the better total (TO: fewer wins).
  • Bar length shows the size of the edge.

Trade analyzer

  • Moves the selected players between the two teams.
  • Recounts category totals with the same 13-player, no-IL rule.
  • Shows category rank changes and power rank (by categories won per week, without the playoff simulation).
  • Flags roster size over 16 and injured players in the deal.

Weekly slate

  • Every NBA game for each rostered player, Monday to Sunday.
  • Games left counts starts from today on.
  • Starts are capped at 10 per day, the size of the starting lineup.
  • Projected week uses the matchup prediction above.

Update schedule

  • Daily at 2:30 PM (Manila), automatic: league data from the Google Sheet (scores, standings, rosters, IL slots, pending trades); injuries; news.
  • Monday: Hashtagbasketball stats refresh, category ranks, power rankings, NBA schedule for the next weeks.
  • League data is pasted into the Google Sheet each week (and after trades or adds/drops). Everything else runs on its own.
